*Spoilers ahead for [The Tragedy of Ravenmere Manor](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/product/572860/The-Tragedy-of-Ravenmere-Manor).*

## Borb

Borbulious walked away from the forges of his homeland to work alongside nature
instead of bending it to his will, which is not a thing dwarves do. He found
Gede, and through her he found he was gifted in The Craft. The kind that comes up out
of the land, not the kind bargained from Shune.

Then Ravenmere. I played Borb as a guest in
[somebody else's game](/hobby/shadowdark-repaired-something-in-me/), a one-shot
run by a paid DM, and I spent the entire night rolling 1s and 6s. All but one
spell failed, the clutch Toadstool cast that saved our pit fighter. Shortly
after, Borb was downed, but my group defeated the big bad before my death timer
ran out, stabilized me, and we all made it back to town.

As far as they are concerned, that finished the job. They split the gold, left
Borb the signet ring and the amulet, and went looking for the next one. Borb
knows better. Lord Ravenmere's ghost named two conditions before the house would
be free of its curse and legally his: lay the family in their crypts, and get
the page of the Necronomicon out of the house. Neither is done. The bodies are
still down there, and so is the page.

Victor Ravenmere wanted that signet ring and did not get it, which makes him the
second problem.

The night he got back to town he drank himself stupid with a dwarf priest of
Gede and told him all of it. Every spell that failed, the house still standing,
the favor he is certain he has lost. The priest listened, and in the morning he
was still there.

So Borb is going back. He has sixteen gold, a frog familiar named Bitter, a
priest who believes he will find his way back to Gede, and a god who has not
said anything since.

## A crew, or the start of one

The priest was up before Borb was, and dressed, and he had slept on it. He was
coming.

That was the part Borb had not expected. He had spent a whole night explaining
why his god had stopped speaking to him, and the one man in the room whose job
is speaking for her heard all of it and signed on anyway.

Two is not a crew. The manor had gone badly with a full company at his back, and
he needed to find more people willing to go in there and put the family to rest.

"I know someone that will help, and I know just where to find him," the priest
said.
