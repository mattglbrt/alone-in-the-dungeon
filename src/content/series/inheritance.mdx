Borbulious came out of Ravenmere Manor with a signet ring, an amulet, and no
magic. The mercenary company he hired on with killed Lady Ravenmere and called
it a job well done. Borb knows the house is still cursed, the family is still
unburied, and the page of the Necronomicon that raised them is still in there
somewhere. Lord Ravenmere's ghost says the manor is his if he finishes it.

Solo actual play using the Shadowdark RPG rules by The Arcane Library, picking
up where a one-shot left off. Nothing scripted, everything emergent from dice,
oracles, and tables.
